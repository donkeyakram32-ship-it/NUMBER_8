Speakers and Call for Papers

Dr. Sarah Kato

Research Director · Uganda Coffee Research Institute

Sarah studies coffee quality and sustainable production systems. Her work connects research findings with practical guidance for farming communities.

Peter Lwanga

Q-Grader and Trainer · Kampala Coffee Collective

Peter trains producers and roasters to assess coffee quality. He works with local teams to strengthen post-harvest handling.

Grace Namara

Agronomist · Green Fields Cooperative

Grace supports farmer groups adopting climate-smart practices. She focuses on soil health, shade management and resilient livelihoods.

Joseph Okello

Processing Specialist · East Africa Coffee Works

Joseph advises cooperatives on processing methods and equipment. His projects help producers protect quality while reducing waste.

Call for Papers

Researchers and practitioners are invited to submit original abstracts on coffee production, processing, trade, climate resilience and innovation. Submissions should include a clear title, a 250-word abstract and the author's name and organisation.

Selected contributors will be contacted by email and may be invited to present a short talk or poster. Please submit materials through the event secretariat; the dates below are sample planning dates for this fictional exercise.
