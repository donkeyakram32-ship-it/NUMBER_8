Uganda International Coffee Expo 2026

16–18 September 2026 · Speke Resort Munyonyo

The Uganda International Coffee Expo brings together growers, processors, buyers and researchers to share ideas across the coffee value chain. This fictional examination event is organised by the Ministry of Agriculture, Animal Industry and Fisheries.

Over three days, visitors can explore new products, learn from practitioners and build connections that support a resilient coffee sector.
